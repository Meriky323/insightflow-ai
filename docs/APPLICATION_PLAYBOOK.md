# InsightFlow — recruiting application playbook

## What to show first

Do not start by explaining SerpAPI, FastAPI, SQLite or Sub2API.

Start with the business system:

**Fragmented overseas signals → auditable evidence → consumer / competitor insight → Product Action + GTM Action + Next Validation.**

## 90-second interview path

1. **15 sec — problem**  
   Overseas product and GTM research is fragmented across reviews, social/community discussion, shopping data and trend tools. AI summaries are fast but often untraceable.

2. **20 sec — product**  
   InsightFlow normalizes source / market / date, structures consumer voice, blocks invalid comparisons, and turns evidence into a prioritized decision hypothesis.

3. **25 sec — core case**  
   Show the magnetic power-bank decision boundary: real evidence supports portability/thermal validation, but the system refuses unsupported US-vs-AU consumer claims.

4. **20 sec — target-company case**  
   Open the Insta360 X6 case. Explain that the same engine identifies a different problem: the hardware spec race is intense, so workflow reliability and time-to-share become the GTM battleground.

5. **10 sec — why it matters**  
   “I built this to practice the exact handoff I want to do in a global business team: evidence → user insight → product / marketing action.”

## Best resume framing

**InsightFlow AI｜Global Consumer & GTM Intelligence Platform**  
Independently designed and built an evidence-backed overseas research workflow integrating public product, community and trend signals; structured unstructured consumer feedback into needs, drivers, barriers, scenarios and purchase-impact signals, with cross-market comparison guardrails and traceable evidence. Built reusable decision cases across consumer electronics categories, translating insights into Product Action, GTM Action and validation plans.

## What not to emphasize

- Do not lead with “crawler” or “scraper”.
- Do not claim representative market sentiment from a small public sample.
- Do not call review count “sales”.
- Do not say AI “predicts the next hit product”.
- Do not present unsupported personas or demographic claims.
- Do not over-explain infrastructure unless an interviewer asks.

## What the project proves

- Overseas consumer / market research thinking
- Data sense and evidence discipline
- Competitive benchmarking
- Product + GTM translation
- AI workflow design
- Ability to build a usable internal-tool prototype end to end
