# Final scope decisions

## Keep
- Google Shopping / Walmart / YouTube / Google Trends live collection
- CSV / JSON evidence import
- OpenAI-compatible AI engine (including local Sub2API)
- Historical topic delta
- Consumer Voice
- Competitive Benchmark
- Market Comparison with comparability gate
- Opportunity Board
- Evidence confidence
- Executive PDF / Evidence CSV
- Recruiter snapshot mode

## Do not add before autumn recruiting
- Instagram scraping
- TikTok full crawler
- social publishing
- influencer CRM
- customer-service inbox
- fake demographic personas
- sales estimation from review volume
- generic “AI predicts the next viral product” claims

These additions increase engineering scope faster than they increase evidence of overseas GTM / consumer-insight ability.

## Best future extension
When fresh data access is available, add **Reddit as a compliant connector** and use TikTok/Instagram through licensed exports / first-party datasets or a compliant provider. The existing Evidence Layer is already designed to accept them without rebuilding the analysis pipeline.
