# 90-second recruiter demo path

The demo should prove **business judgement**, not every feature.

## 0-15s — Portfolio landing

Say:

> “I built InsightFlow because overseas research usually stops at collecting information. I wanted a workflow that keeps evidence traceable and converts it into Product + GTM actions.”

Click **Explore the flagship case**.

## 15-35s — Executive Snapshot

Show the **Decision Memo** first.

Key message:

> “The system does not start with a dashboard. It starts with the business question, shows confidence, then gives Product Action, GTM Action and Next Validation.”

Point out that the saved demo is intentionally compact and confidence is not hidden.

## 35-50s — Trend Radar

Show historical topic delta.

Say:

> “I separate live search momentum from historical consumer-topic change. Google Trends is normalized, so I never compare US=80 and AU=40 as if US demand were 2x.”

## 50-65s — Consumer Voice / Competitive Benchmark

Open one evidence link, then show competitor cards.

Say:

> “Each insight can return to a source. Competitor consumer evidence uses conservative entity linkage and keeps GLOBAL evidence GLOBAL.”

## 65-80s — Market Comparison

This is the most important screen.

Say:

> “The demo has US and AU product data, but consumer voice is mostly GLOBAL, so the system blocks a US-vs-AU preference chart. I think refusing an unsupported conclusion is more valuable than generating a polished but wrong visualization.”

## 80-90s — Opportunity Board

Show one card with Product / GTM / Next Validation.

Close with:

> “The output is not ‘AI says users care about heat’. It is: here is the evidence, here is the confidence, here is the business implication, and here is the next experiment I would run.”
