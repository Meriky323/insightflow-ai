# InsightFlow 2.1 — Design Reference Notes

The production direction is not a pixel-copy of any site. References were reduced to reusable principles:

- **Linear**: lower visual noise, consistent navigation/control geometry, main content takes priority over chrome.
- **Raycast**: contextual command interaction and one obvious primary action.
- **Vercel / Geist**: typography hierarchy, high-contrast accessibility, spacing and grid discipline.
- **React Bits**: Magnet, Spotlight Card, Scroll Stack, Tilted Card, text reveal and animated background ideas. The production implementation is dependency-free CSS/JS to avoid a React migration risk.
- **MotionSites**: strong art direction first; motion is scroll-linked and composition-led instead of “animate everything”.
- **Meltwater**: AI insight must click through to underlying conversations for human validation.
- **Sprinklr Product Insights**: omnichannel feedback + AI theme/root-cause analysis + recommended actions is the correct business workflow.

## Production mix

**Evidence Editorial** supplies the page rhythm, typography and traceability motif.
**Signal Lab** contributes a small amount of kinetic, technical energy to the hero and workspace states.
**Field Notes** contributes the quieter methodological tone used for guardrails and case-study sections.

The result should feel designed by someone who understands both research and product interaction, not like a component-library showcase.
