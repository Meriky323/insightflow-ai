# InsightFlow AI v1.4 — Final recruiter-ready delivery

## What to show a recruiter

1. `/` — recruiter landing
2. `/?demo=1` — flagship executive workspace
3. `Market Comparison` — strongest data-sense guardrail
4. `Opportunity Board` — Product Action + GTM Action + Next Validation
5. `/case-study.html` — full business narrative

Recommended demo time: **90 seconds**. See `docs/RECRUITER_DEMO_PATH.md`.

## What to put on a resume

Use `PORTFOLIO_COPY.md`. Do not describe the project as a crawler. The strongest positioning is:

> Overseas Consumer Intelligence + AI + data-driven GTM decision workflow.

## What is intentionally excluded

TikTok/Instagram full crawlers, social publishing, fake persona generation, sales estimation from review counts, and unsupported AI forecasting. These add scope without strengthening the core recruiting story.
