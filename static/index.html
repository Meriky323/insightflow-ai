REAL WORKFLOW, NOT A CHATBOT

A research system designed around the decisions teams actually make.

The model is useful only after the evidence is normalized, scoped and made comparable.

01

Frame the business question

Product launch, GTM, competitor intelligence or messaging — research starts from a decision, not a keyword.

02

Build an evidence layer

Shopping, retail reviews, YouTube, Trends and imported datasets share one auditable schema with source, market and date boundaries.

03

Structure consumer voice

AI extracts multi-label topics, drivers, barriers, scenarios and purchase impact. Fallback logic remains explicit when AI is unavailable.

04

Turn signals into action

Trend change + consumer evidence + competitor coverage becomes an opportunity hypothesis with Product Action, GTM Action and Next Validation.

FLAGSHIP WALKTHROUGH

One category. Two periods. A deliberately honest decision boundary.

The demo asks a simple business question: what should a 10K magnetic power bank prove before launch, and which market claims are not yet supported?

Signal

Decision

Method & decision guardrails

What the system can infer — and what it intentionally refuses to claim.

01 · Evidence

Source before summary

Every consumer row retains source, market, date/window status and link. Missing data is shown as a gap.

02 · AI

Structure, not fabricate

LLM extracts topics, drivers, barriers and scenarios from supplied evidence. When unavailable, fallback analysis is explicitly labeled.

03 · Trends

Direction ≠ market size

Google Trends 0–100 is normalized. Separately requested US/AU indices are not treated as absolute demand or market-size ratios.

04 · Markets

Comparable sources only

Cross-market consumer claims are blocked unless the same consumer-voice source covers every requested market.

05 · Opportunity

Hypothesis, not TAM

Opportunity scores prioritize what to validate next. They are not revenue forecasts, market-share estimates or proof of PMF.

06 · Public demo

No secret or quota exposure

Recruiter mode uses a saved traceable snapshot. Live research, uploads, connector diagnostics and paid LLM calls stay disabled.
