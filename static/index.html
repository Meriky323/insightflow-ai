METHOD & GUARDRAILS

What the system can — and cannot — claim

01

Evidence, not synthetic rows

If a live source returns nothing, InsightFlow shows missing coverage instead of inventing consumer comments.

02

Review count ≠ sales

Retail review counts and rankings are signals only. They are never converted into fake unit sales.

03

Market comparison requires comparable sources

GLOBAL Reddit or YouTube evidence cannot be assigned to US or AU without reliable geography.

04

Trend index ≠ market size

Google Trends is normalized within each request. The system uses direction and change, not raw indices as country-size comparisons.

05

Opportunity = validation priority

Scores decide what to test next. They do not prove whitespace, TAM or product-market fit.

06

Public demo protects quota

Recruiters explore a saved evidence snapshot. Live API research, uploads and secret settings stay disabled.
